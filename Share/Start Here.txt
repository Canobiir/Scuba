WELCOME TO SCUBA  (0.5 beta)
============================

Scuba lives in your menu bar (the ⊞ icon) and arranges your windows into
pools. Any pool can open up into a whole desktop of its own, so instead
of shuffling windows around one flat screen, you dive down into your work
and come back up for air.

This is a beta, so a few things may be rough. Thanks for trying it!
For a quick tour, open the ⊞ menu and choose Quick guide…

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 (Sonoma) or
newer. A trackpad makes it a lot more fun.


1. INSTALL
----------
1. Drag Scuba into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS will say it can't check the app for malware,
   because test builds don't come from the App Store. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Scuba. Confirm with your
   password or Touch ID.

   If macOS says the app is "damaged", open Terminal, paste this line and
   press Return, then open Scuba again:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Scuba.app


2. PERMISSIONS
--------------
A Setup window opens the first time.

- Accessibility (needed): click "Turn On…", then switch Scuba on in the list.
  Scuba restarts by itself.
- Screen Recording (optional): this only powers the smooth zoom animation.
  Switch it on, then click "Restart App".
- Your browser (later): the first time you save a pool with Safari or
  Chrome in it, macOS asks whether Scuba may control that browser. Choose
  Allow. That's how Scuba remembers your tabs. The tabs aren't saved that
  first time, so save the pool again once you've allowed it.

You can reopen Setup anytime from the ⊞ menu.


3. ONE TRACKPAD SETTING
-----------------------
Scuba uses three-finger swipes, and so does macOS. Go to System Settings ›
Trackpad › More Gestures and set "Swipe between full-screen applications"
and "Mission Control" to four fingers. Then three fingers are Scuba's.


4. SMALLER SCREENS (13" AND 14" MACBOOKS)
-----------------------------------------
Most apps won't shrink below a certain window size, so on a smaller
screen, pools inside pools quickly get too small to hold real windows.
Those windows show as portholes instead: a frosted picture of the
window, cropped at its pool's edge. Click one to dive to its board, or
Hyper + click it to blow the window up right where you are.

To try portholes that show the whole window, shrunk and updating: ⊞ menu
› Settings › Windows & pools › Windows too big for a nested pool ›
Live portholes (BETA).

For more room for real, usable windows, run your display at its highest
resolution with BetterDisplay (github.com/waydabber/BetterDisplay).
Everything on screen gets smaller, but nested pools fit more real
windows. On a MacBook's built-in screen, the highest resolutions may
need BetterDisplay Pro, which has a 14-day free trial.

No install? System Settings › Displays › "More Space" gets you part of
the way there.


5. HOW TO SWIM
--------------
"Hyper" means holding Control + Option + Command together. There are
only a few Hyper keys on purpose; everything else is in the ⊞ menu.

Getting in and out
  Hyper + ↑              Dive in from your normal desktop to Main
  Hyper + Esc            Surface: back to your normal desktop, windows and all
  Three fingers down     Dive into the pool under the pointer
  Three fingers up       Come back up a level
  Three fingers sideways Slide over to the next board on the same level
  (No trackpad? Hyper + scroll over a pool, and Hyper + ↓ to come back up.)

Putting windows in place
  Open an app while you're inside, and its window joins the board.
  Drag a window to an edge (of the screen or another window) to dock it.
  Drop it on the middle of another window to tab them together.
  Hyper + ← / →          Snap the window you're using to that half
  Hyper + ↑ / ↓          ...then make it the top / bottom quarter
  Drag the pill in a gap Resize the windows on either side

Handy extras
  Hyper + B              Breathing room: an empty corner to dive into.
                         Leave it empty and it folds away when you go up.
  Hyper + Y              Tile that breathing room into the board instead
  Hyper + F              Frost or unfrost a window too small to use
  Hyper + Shift + 1–9    Save the view on screen; Hyper + 1–9 comes back
  Hyper + Z              Undo the last layout change
  Hyper + /              Show every shortcut


6. HIDING, MOVING, LOCKING AND SAVING
------------------------------------
Hiding things when you zoom out
  Hyper + H              Hide the window under the pointer from the layer
                         above. From above it's gone and its neighbours
                         spread out; dive back down and it's right there.
                         Again shows it.
  Hyper + Shift + H      The same for the whole pool under the pointer

Moving a window anywhere (tabs come along)
  Hyper + X              Cut the window you're using. A chip at the bottom
                         of the screen shows what you're holding.
  Hyper + C              Copy it instead (pasted, it's in both pools)
  Hyper + V              Go anywhere, point at a pool and paste it there

Locking
  Hyper + P              Lock this board: nothing docks, snaps or resizes,
                         and new windows float on top. Again unlocks.
  Hyper + L              Lock the window under the pointer in place

Saving a pool for later (BETA)
  In the ⊞ menu under Saved pools: save the pool you're in, save it and
  close it, or open a saved one into an empty pool. Each app opens and its
  window lands where it was. Browsers come back with their tabs; other apps
  open a fresh window in the right spot.


7. GETTING OUT
--------------
⊞ menu › Quit Scuba puts every window back where you can see it. If
anything gets weird, quit and reopen it.
